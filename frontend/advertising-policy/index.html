Last updated: September 29, 2026

Advertising never changes the scientific information, search results or ordering on ViaDrug.

1. Clear labeling

Advertisements appear only in the dedicated advertising areas of the website, labeled "Advertisement" and clearly separated from scientific content. We aim to show advertisements related to pharmacy and healthcare.

2. Editorial independence

Advertisers cannot pay to change, remove or promote any scientific content, ranking or result. Advertising does not mean we endorse a product or service.

3. What we do not accept

  • Advertisements promoting prescription-only medicines to the general public.
  • Misleading, exaggerated or unsubstantiated health claims, including claims of cure.
  • Unregistered, counterfeit or unlawful products or services.
  • Advertisements that target children with medicines or health products.

4. Compliance

Advertisers are responsible for complying with the Egyptian laws and regulations that govern advertising of health products. We may ask for proof of registration or approval before publishing, and we may refuse or remove any advertisement at our discretion.

5. Privacy

Advertising partners may use technologies that process technical data. We will describe them in our Privacy Policy and request consent where required before activating them.

6. Report an advertisement
